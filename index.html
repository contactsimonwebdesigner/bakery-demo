About the Project ↗
Is MOU. a real patisserie? +
No. MOU. is a fictional brand and demo design project showing a modern patisserie website. You cannot make real purchases here.
Can I order a cake through this site? +
You can try the dessert selection and cake builder and copy your choices, but no order is placed or paid for.
Are the listed prices valid? +
No, all prices in USD are illustrative. A real patisserie would set its own prices, availability and allergen information.
How could I get a website like this? +
This site demonstrates the possibilities of interactive web design. A live site would be tailored to the business's brand, products, photos and ordering process.
